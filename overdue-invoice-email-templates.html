Overdue invoice email templates: friendly, firm and final (free to copy)

2026-09-20

Chasing late payment is uncomfortable, so it gets put off, and the longer you wait the harder it gets. A simple three-step escalation removes most of the awkwardness: a friendly reminder, a firm follow-up, then a final notice. Decide the timing in advance and it becomes routine rather than personal.

When to use which

Days past dueToneGoal
1-14FriendlyAssume it was missed. Make it easy to pay.
15-44FirmAsk for a specific payment date. Invite queries.
45+FinalState the outstanding amount and a deadline. Decide the next step yourself.

Tier by the oldest unpaid invoice for that customer, and list every overdue invoice in one email rather than sending several. If a normally prompt customer is 12 days late, a lighter touch than the table suggests is usually right.

Template 1: friendly (1-14 days)

Subject: Quick reminder - invoice {{invoice_numbers}}

Hi {{contact_name}},

A quick note that the following invoice(s) are now {{days_overdue}} days past the due date:

{{invoice_table}}

Total outstanding: {{total}}

If it has already been paid, thank you - please ignore this and let me know so I can match it. If anything is holding it up, just tell me and I will help sort it out.

Kind regards,
{{sender_name}}

Template 2: firm (15-44 days)

Subject: Overdue: invoice {{invoice_numbers}} - {{total}}

Hi {{contact_name}},

I am following up on invoice(s) {{invoice_numbers}}, now {{days_overdue}} days overdue:

{{invoice_table}}

Total outstanding: {{total}}

Could you confirm by {{reply_by_date}} when payment will be made? If there is a query on the invoice, please let me know the details today so we can resolve it quickly.

Kind regards,
{{sender_name}}

Template 3: final notice (45+ days)

Subject: Final notice: {{invoice_numbers}} - {{total}} overdue {{days_overdue}} days

Dear {{contact_name}},

Despite earlier reminders, the following remains unpaid, {{days_overdue}} days past its due date:

{{invoice_table}}

Total outstanding: {{total}}

Please arrange payment or contact me to agree a plan by {{reply_by_date}}. {{next_step}}

Regards,
{{sender_name}}

Before you send the final notice

  • Check it is true. If the template says "despite earlier reminders", make sure earlier reminders were actually sent.
  • Decide the next step yourself (a late-fee clause, pausing work, a collections agency) and only state what your contract allows. Do not threaten anything you will not or cannot do.
  • Rules on late-payment fees and interest differ by country and by whether the customer is a business or a consumer. Check what applies where you operate, and take advice before escalating.
  • Keep a record. Save what you sent and when.
